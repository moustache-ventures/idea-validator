# PDF Summarizer — Idea

## Elevator Pitch

Everyone reads too much. Nobody has time.

Every professional — lawyer, accountant, student, analyst — spends hours reading documents that could be understood in minutes. They know it's a waste of time. They just don't have a better option.

We give them one: upload any PDF, pick a style (summary, bullet points, or ELI5), get the answer in 10 seconds. No signup friction — pay $5, get 10 credits, done.

The market is enormous and the ad copy writes itself: "50 pages. 10 seconds. $0.50." We target Google search ("summarize PDF online") where intent is crystal clear and CPC is low. We don't need to convince anyone they have a problem — they're already searching for the solution.

Build cost: ~$200. Break-even: 40 purchases. At $5 a pop with a $1k/month ad budget, that's achievable in week one.

The risk is competition — there are free tools. Our edge: speed of experience, output quality via Claude, and ELI5 mode which free tools don't offer. We don't need to win the whole market, just carve a niche worth $1–2k/month.

Invest or pass?

## Key Details
- **Product**: Web app for PDF summarization
- **Pricing**: $5 for 10 credits (~$0.50/summary)
- **Output modes**: Summary, bullet points, ELI5
- **Distribution**: Google Search SEO/SEM ("summarize PDF online")
- **Build cost estimate**: ~$200
- **Break-even**: 40 purchases
- **Target revenue**: $1–2k/month
- **AI backbone**: Claude API
- **Differentiation**: Speed, quality (Claude), ELI5 mode
