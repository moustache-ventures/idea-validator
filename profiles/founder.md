# Founder Profile — Hard Constraints

## Who I am

Solo developer building a portfolio of 20+ micro-SaaS products. Goal: $1k–$5k MRR per product, compounding over time. Not chasing unicorns — looking for boring, profitable, low-maintenance software that quietly generates revenue.

## Tech stack

AWS CDK v2 (TypeScript), Node.js 20 Lambda, DynamoDB single-table design, React 18 + Vite + Tailwind + shadcn/ui, Stripe, Anthropic Claude API. Can ship a standard SaaS MVP in 2–4 weeks.

## Marketing reality — hard constraint

**Available channels:**
- Personal Facebook/Instagram account (not a brand page — cold ads are viable but building a following is not)
- Cold email outbound (if a list of target customers is obtainable)
- SEO (long-tail, low-competition keywords — have time to write a few pages, not an ongoing content operation)
- Directory listings (G2, Capterra, Product Hunt, industry directories — one-time submissions)
- B2B partnerships with complementary tools (if they come inbound or are easy to cold-pitch)

**Not available — permanently:**
- Reddit (no account, no karma, no community standing)
- Twitter/X (no following)
- LinkedIn (no network)
- Hacker News (no account)
- YouTube / podcast / newsletter (not building these)
- Niche Slack/Discord communities (not joining 20 communities for 20 products)

**Implication for idea selection:** any idea where the only realistic path to first 10 customers is community building or audience growth is a non-starter for this portfolio.

## Revenue targets

| Target | Status |
|---|---|
| $300/mo | Acceptable only if build time < 1 week |
| $1k–$3k/mo | Good — worth building |
| $3k–$5k/mo | Great |
| $5k+/mo | Unlikely to hit solo without marketing investment — not the goal |

## Hard no categories

- Legal, medical, financial, or tax advice (even "AI-assisted")
- Products requiring HIPAA compliance or PCI DSS card data handling
- Two-sided marketplaces (chicken-and-egg, fraud, trust — too complex solo)
- Physical goods or hardware
- UGC at scale (moderation liability)
- Platform scraping that violates ToS
- Anything that requires hiring (even contractors) to function

## Soft no categories (not automatic kill, but need strong compensating factors)

- Pure AI wrappers (thin Claude API call with no data moat, no workflow lock-in — 12-month shelf life)
- B2B enterprise (long sales cycles, procurement, procurement delays)
- Consumer social apps (require viral loop or massive paid spend)
- Compliance-heavy niches even if not regulated (financial services adjacent, healthcare adjacent)
- Ideas requiring 6+ months to first revenue

## What I'm looking for

- Unsexy, boring problems that small businesses have always had
- Clear buyer (someone who has a credit card and a specific pain)
- Acquisition via SEO or ads or cold outreach — not community
- Low maintenance per customer at scale
- Something AI makes 10x better than the pre-AI alternative, not just "also AI"
